Hōʻike Explorer

Personal · Hawaiʻi · OpenAI x Handshake Codex Creator Challenge

A personal prototype addressing the Hawaiʻi brain drain through hidden curriculum research, built with OpenAI Codex. It translates a student's activity into local pathways, simulated Local Voices, and small next steps, and imagines a digital network connecting educators, professionals, and learners across the diaspora. Matching is rule-based (no AI model calls at runtime) and the professional profiles are simulated. An exploratory imagining, not a deployed product.
