Hi, I'm Grace.

I'm an ʻIolani '24 and Santa Clara University '28 student with a love for learning. Raised in Hawaiʻi as a Chinese~American adoptee and now studying in Silicon Valley, I major in General Engineering with a self-designed concentration in culturally sustaining and accessible STEM learning.

I have an extensive background in early childhood and elementary education and competitive extracurricular STEM leadership in and out of the classroom.

I'm actively exploring EdResearch and EdTech through a couple of research labs. In the Human-Computer Interaction Lab, I ask how technology can facilitate stories and relationships, and how user perspectives influence this. In the RESS Lab, I look into how cultural backgrounds influence minorities in STEM higher ed.

If I'm not doing homework or in the HCI / RESSL labs, I'm probably running, cooking, or playing the tenor sax :)
